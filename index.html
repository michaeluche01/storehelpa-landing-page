Every Module Connected

From opening float to locked Close Day — built for real shop floors.

Your shop, in good hands.
SELL · POS

Scan-first point of sale

Scan unit or carton barcodes with a USB/Bluetooth HID scanner or phone camera, or work hands-on-keyboard with F2 search, F4 charge, F6 hold, and F8 new cart. Hold and resume carts across cashiers, record cash, bank transfer, POS terminal, or split tenders, print 58mm/80mm receipts, or share via WhatsApp.

58mm & 80mm thermal WhatsApp receipts Approved voids & returns
STOCK CONTROL

Movement-backed stock

On-hand stock is always the sum of every recorded movement. Receive supplier stock with carton-to-unit conversion, log damage adjustments with reasons, run partial stock-takes without zeroing uncounted items, and print shelf barcode labels.

FEFO & ALERTS

Earliest-expiry first

Dated batches allocate earliest-expiry first at checkout, while expired batches are blocked automatically. Low-stock, near-expiry, and expired alerts surface before you lose a sale or stock.

MONEY · CLOSE DAY

Cash, expenses & locked Close Day

Track opening float, staff stock/cash/expense requests, and photo-backed expenses. Close Day steps through attendance, stock, sales, and counted cash — locking the day with an immutable snapshot. Variances or reopening require an Owner PIN.

INSIGHTS · AUDIT

Immutable reports & full audit trail

Generate numbered Day, Month, and completed-Year report snapshots that reopen unchanged even if product names or settings change later. Print clean A4 sheets, export CSV or Excel-compatible .xls tables, and search a read-only audit trail showing who did what, when, and on which device.

Frozen A4 PDF sheets CSV & .xls exports Every device & actor logged
